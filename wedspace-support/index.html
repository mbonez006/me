Wedspace Support

Need a hand with planning, your wedding team, invitations, or something in the app? Find a quick answer below or get in touch with our support team.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get help with my account or signing in?

Email us from the address associated with your Wedspace account if possible. Tell us what happens when you try to sign in and include any error message. Never send your password or a sign-in verification code.

How can I invite someone to my wedding workspace?

Workspace owners and members with the appropriate access can manage their wedding team from within the app. If an invitation is not arriving or someone cannot join, contact us with the wedding workspace name and the email address or phone number used for the invitation.

I'm having trouble with a guest invitation or RSVP.

Let us know whether you are organising the wedding or responding as a guest, what you were trying to do, and what happened instead. Please avoid sending guest personal information unless it is needed to identify the issue.

My plans or changes aren't appearing on another device.

Check that you are signed in to the correct account and connected to the internet, then reopen Wedspace. If the issue continues, email us with the affected device(s), approximate time, and the type of information that is not appearing. Do not include private wedding details in the subject line.

I need help with a Wedding Pass or purchase.

Contact us with the Wedspace account email, the Wedding Pass or product involved, and the approximate purchase date. Do not email payment-card details, passwords, or verification codes. For billing or refund decisions handled by Apple, use Apple's purchase support options.

How do I delete my account?

You can start an account deletion request in the app or by email. The deletion page explains the steps and what happens to wedding workspaces and shared information.

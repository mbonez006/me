A few things you might be wondering

Can we try Wedspace before buying anything?

Yes. Your checklist, budget, first 50 guests and guest photo uploads are free. Wedding Pass is optional and unlocks additional tools for your wedding.

Can our guests RSVP without making an account?

Guest invitations are designed to be account-free. Guests install Wedspace, choose the guest invitation option and enter the private code shared with them to view their invitation and RSVP.

Is Wedspace available on iPhone and Android?

Wedspace is a mobile app for iOS and Android. Use the store links above to check availability for your device and region.

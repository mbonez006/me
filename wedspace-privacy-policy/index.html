1. Who we are and what this notice covers

Wedspace is a wedding-planning app operated by OLUASHIRU LTD, a company established in the United Kingdom. OLUASHIRU LTD is the data controller for personal information described in this notice.

This notice applies to the Wedspace mobile app and its account-free wedding guest invitation experience. It does not describe separate websites or services that may be linked from the app.

Wedspace is offered worldwide, with a focus on the United Kingdom and Europe. Where local privacy laws give you additional rights, those rights continue to apply.

2. Information we handle

The information we process depends on the features you and the wedding organiser choose to use.

  • Account and membership information: email address, account and authentication identifiers, profile or display name, optional phone number, and wedding-team membership, roles and permissions.
  • Wedding workspace information: wedding date and time zone; planning notes, checklists, budgets, supplier details, schedules, design ideas and other information entered into the workspace.
  • Guest and invitation information: names, household details, contact details supplied by the organiser, RSVP answers, invitation status and guest-provided responses. This can include dietary needs or allergies. Some allergy information may reveal health information, so organisers should only add what is needed and ensure they are allowed to share it.
  • Photos and other media: images added to an invitation or wedding photo collection, and images or voice recordings sent in wedding chats. Voice notes are recorded only when you choose the voice-note feature.
  • Messages and collaboration: chat text and media, group membership, and the information needed to show, deliver and manage conversations.
  • Notifications and purchases: a push-notification token for a device where you enable notifications, notification delivery preferences available to us, and limited purchase and transaction references used to verify a Wedding Pass.

Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device. Depending on the app configuration, some app data may also be stored locally on the device before or instead of being synchronised to a cloud wedding workspace.

A wedding organiser may enter information about other people, including guests who do not have a Wedspace account. The organiser should make sure they have a lawful reason to provide that information and share this notice with the people concerned.

3. How and why we use information

We use information to provide the features you request and operate Wedspace, including to:

  • create and secure accounts, authenticate members and manage access to wedding workspaces;
  • save, organise and share wedding plans, guest lists, RSVP information and other workspace content with authorised members;
  • provide guest invitations, RSVP features and wedding photo collections;
  • deliver chats, voice notes and notifications, and keep messages available only for the published retention window;
  • verify purchases and provide the Wedding Pass features associated with them;
  • protect the service, prevent misuse, resolve support requests and meet legal obligations.

For users protected by UK or European data-protection law, the applicable legal basis will generally be performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, your consent where a feature or law requires it, or compliance with a legal obligation. You can turn off optional push notifications in your device settings. Where dietary or allergy notes reveal health information, we use them only to provide the wedding-planning functionality and where an applicable legal condition permits that processing.

We do not include third-party in-app analytics or crash-reporting services in the released Wedspace app.

4. When information is shared

Wedspace is designed for collaboration. Workspace information is available to members according to the roles and permissions set for that wedding. Guest invitation information is available to the organiser and authorised wedding members; guest-facing details and photo visibility depend on the invitation and album features in use.

We use service providers to operate the app:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): account authentication and cloud hosting, APIs, workspace storage, private media storage and real-time chat infrastructure. The live backend is hosted in the AWS Europe (Ireland) region, eu-west-1.
  • Expo: push-notification delivery. Expo forwards notifications to Apple Push Notification service (APNs) for iOS and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for Android. Push notifications do not include message text or media previews.
  • Apple and Google: in-app purchase processing and verification for the Wedding Pass on their respective platforms. Payment card and billing details are handled by the relevant store, not by Wedspace.

If you choose to prepare an invitation for sharing by SMS or a messaging app, that action is completed through the app you choose on your device. That provider’s own privacy terms apply to information you send through it.

We do not sell personal information. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer, subject to applicable law.

5. International processing

The Wedspace backend is hosted in Ireland. Some service providers, including push-notification and app-store providers, may process limited information in other countries to deliver their services. When personal information is transferred internationally, OLUASHIRU LTD will apply transfer safeguards required by applicable data-protection law.

6. How long we keep information

  • Wedding workspaces: ordinary planning records do not have an automatic expiry date. They remain until the workspace owner deletes the wedding workspace. An account holder deleting their account can transfer an owned workspace to an existing member or choose to delete it.
  • Guest photos: uploads are open from 90 days before a wedding through 7 days after it. Photos become unavailable and are scheduled for deletion 97 days after the wedding. The app and API stop serving expired photos at that time; removal of stored objects by cloud lifecycle processes may happen asynchronously.
  • Wedding chats and media: chat content becomes unavailable three calendar months after the wedding date captured when the first chat group is created. Stored message and media deletion is performed asynchronously after the service stops serving the content.
  • Account deletion: deleting an account removes the account and its access. If you are a member of a workspace owned by someone else, your membership is removed but the workspace remains for its owner and other members. If you own a workspace, you must transfer it or choose to delete it for everyone before account removal can complete.

Copies that other members have downloaded, exported or shared outside Wedspace, and records held independently by Apple or Google, are not deleted by deleting your Wedspace account or workspace.

7. Security

We use access controls and cloud security features to protect information. The AWS services used for workspace and media storage provide encryption at rest, and app-to-service connections use encrypted network connections. Access to shared information is limited by wedding membership and feature permissions.

Wedding chat content is not end-to-end encrypted. Authorised backend systems and privileged Wedspace administrators may be able to access stored chat content to operate and support the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You can manage workspace information through the app, change notification permissions in your device settings, and request account deletion from the app. Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may also have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, to restrict or object to some processing, to receive certain information in a portable format, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact oluashiru@outlook.com. We may need to verify your identity and may ask an organiser to handle a request about information they provided for their wedding workspace.

If you are in the United Kingdom, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). If you are in another country or region, you may contact your local data-protection authority.

9. Children and guest invitations

Wedspace accounts are for people aged 18 or older. Under-18 guests may use an account-free wedding invitation. An adult should provide and manage information about younger children, and should only submit information they are authorised to provide.

If you believe information about a child has been submitted improperly, contact us using the details below and we will review the request.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this notice as Wedspace changes or when legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. If a change materially affects how information is used, we will provide an appropriate notice in the app or by another suitable means.
